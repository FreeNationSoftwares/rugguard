RugGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

RugGuard: Scam Token Checker ("RugGuard", "we") is published by FreeNation Softwares. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

No account, no personal data

RugGuard does not require sign-up and does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or wallet private keys. We do not run our own servers that store your information.

Data stored on your device

Your scan history, watchlist, tracked wallet addresses and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Requests to public data services

To produce a report, the app sends the token or wallet address you enter directly from your phone to public blockchain data services: DexScreener, GoPlus Security, Honeypot.is, RugCheck, Solana RPC providers and Blockscout. These are public addresses on public blockchains. Those services may log technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Alerts are generated on your device by a background check. No push-notification server is used.

Children

RugGuard is intended for adults (18+).

Not financial advice

RugGuard provides automated risk information from public on-chain data. It may be incomplete or wrong and is not financial advice.
